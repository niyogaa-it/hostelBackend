RULES AND REGULATIONS FOR HOSTEL RESIDENTS

ADMISSION PROCEDURE

  1. Students can apply for admission to the hostel on the website - www.ranimeyyammaihostel.org or email at info@ranimeyyammaihostel.org

  2. The shortlisted applicants will be notified at least three days before the date of the interview.

  3. The applicant should be accompanied by the parents and the local guardian for the interview.

  4. If the parents of the applicants are not based in Chennai, a guardian must be nominated on their behalf. During the admission interview, the hostel authority will verify that the profile of the guardian meets the eligibility requirement of the hostel.

  5. On acceptance, the reservation fee must be paid. The balance payment can be paid at the time of check-in at the hostel.

  6. Room category and group preferences can be submitted to the warden during the interview. Allotment of rooms is based on availability. The management's decision is final in the allotment of rooms.

  7. Permission from the warden is mandatory to occupy or interchange rooms.

  8. Admission is renewable every year and is at the discretion of the management.

FEES AND CAUTION DEPOSIT

  1. The fees once paid will not be refunded even if a resident leaves the hostel (voluntarily or involuntarily) during the academic year. There will be no exceptions.

  2. Caution Deposit:

    1. The caution deposit should be paid with the fee on admission.

    2. If there is damage to the hostel property by the student, then the cost of repair/replacement will be adjusted against this deposit.

    3. If the student leaves the hostel midway through the year, the caution deposit will be refunded at the end of the year after deductions, if applicable.
    4. In cases there are no damages, then the amount will be refunded in full.

HOSTEL REQUIREMENTS

  1. Residents are requested to bring personal items like bucket with a mug and lock for the room.

  2. Upon admission, each hostel resident will receive:

    1. An identity card

    2. Bedding essentials:

      • A mattress

      • A pillow

      • Two bedsheets

      • Two pillow covers

    3. A laundry bag

    4.  Basic dining items:

      • A plate

      • A tumbler

  3. The residents are responsible for the safety of their money, valuables, and belongings. The hostel will not be held responsible for any loss.

  4. Residents are required to check and sign the checklist of the furniture and fittings and the general condition of the room at the time of check in.

DRESS CODE

Students should avoid wearing shorts, mini-skirts, and revealing clothes in the dining hall and common areas.

TIPPING POLICY

  • We do not encourage direct tips to our support providers or staff.

  • Please do not pay individually for any services you avail.

  • If you wish to show appreciation, use the tip box provided. The accumulated amount will be distributed to all staff members who have assisted you.

  • Your feedback is essential for us to understand your challenges, concerns, and suggestions.

  • Please use the app to reach out and provide your feedback.

PROPERTY MAINTENANCE

  1. To avoid fire hazards residents are not allowed to cook or keep equipment like an induction stove, iron box, water kettle, etc. in the room.

  2. Residents are expected to maintain the cleanliness and tidiness of their rooms.

  3. All wet clothes should be dried only in the allotted areas and not in the room.

Meal Timings and Dining Room Protocols

  • Breakfast: 7:30 a.m. to 9:30 a.m.
  • Lunch: 10:30 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. (Sunday: 11:00 a.m. to 2:30 p.m.)
  • Snacks: 4:30 p.m. to 6:30 p.m.
  • Dinner: 7:30 p.m. to 9:00 p.m.

Dining Policy

  1. Carrying food to rooms is prohibited; dining areas must be used.
  2. Cooperation with these rules is appreciated.

Additional Servings

  • Coupons are available for extra servings of egg or chicken.
  • Additional servings' costs will be added to the monthly bill.

Entry and Exit

  1. Residents must return to the hostel by 7:00 p.m. without special permission. Violations incur a fine of ₹500/day or restriction of further outings.
  2. Daily entry and exit must be recorded on the biometric system.
  3. Weekends away require a permission letter from parents/guardians.
  4. Written permission must be submitted three days in advance for weekend/holiday visits.
  5. In emergencies, the local guardian can seek permission in person.
  6. Residents must register check-out and check-in timings at the gate.
  7. Strict action will be taken against those staying out without prior permission.
  8. The management will not be responsible for mishaps outside hostel premises.
  9. Visiting Hours: 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. (Special circumstances require warden permission).
  10. Students must open couriers/packages in front of the warden for security reasons.
  11. Residents should take valuables with them during long holidays. Hostel management is not responsible for missing items.
  12. In emergencies like natural calamities or political unrest, belongings left behind will be packed and kept safe. Valuables are discouraged from being left behind.
  13. Hostel closes for maintenance in May. Residents must check out by April 30th.

CONSEQUENCES FOR BREACH OF RULES

  1. To ensure that the hostel has a safe environment for all our residents, we are listing some serious offences that will entail suspension or expulsion from the hostel. The list includes:

    1. Absence from the hostel for more than a month without prior permission.
    2. Forging of parent's/guardian's signature
    3. Giving false information about their whereabouts
    4. Receiving unauthorized visitors
    5. Ragging or bullying - Body shaming, use of abusive language, interference in other people’s space or acts without their consent may be treated as an act of bullying and will be taken under the consequences laid for bullying.
    6. Use of abusive language against the hostel, mess, or administrative staff - Videography, photography, audio recording without permission of residents is a punishable offence and may lead to expulsion.
    7. Aggression or abuse against other hostel residents.
    8. Organizing unauthorized meetings, collecting money, or circulating notices or petitions without prior permission from the Warden.
    9. Gambling, strikes, processions, group fasts, instigating the residents.
    10. Any form of cybercrime that includes the resident's computer or smartphone as either a tool or a target for harassment using email, cyberstalking, defamation, hacking, SMS spoofing, pornography, threat, distribution of pirated software, possession of unauthorized information, cyber terrorism, etc. This includes the use of social media for malicious intent.
    11. Distribution or consumption of cigarettes, narcotics, or alcohol in the hostel premises.
    12. Drug abuse/Drug peddling.
    13. Vandalism in any form inside the hostel premises.
    14. Possession or use of electronic equipment like induction stove, iron box, water kettle, etc. in the rooms.

  2. The hostel authority reserves the right to conduct routine checks either informed or uninformed to ensure that the rules are being adhered to. In case of non-compliance, appropriate disciplinary action will be taken which can include warnings, fines, deduction of damages or/and expulsion.
